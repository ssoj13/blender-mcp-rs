# Blender 5.3: Cycles

## Shader Nodes
### Principled BSDF
* Dispersion is added to the Transmission panel. This can be used for rendering glass or diamonds that split white light into rainbow colors. (blender/blender#162041)

## Denoising
* On NVIDIA GPUs, a new DLSS denoiser is available for the viewport. It provides a cleaner image faster through upscaling and reusing samples from previous frames. (blender/blender#153077)

  At the time of writing, this feature is enabled in Blender daily builds but requires an NVIDIA driver that has not been released yet.

## Others

* Viewport renders are automatically paused during final renders. (blender/blender#156536)
* Texture baking quality improved for small or thin triangles and near margins. (blender/blender#161752)
