.. _bpy.ops.transform.vert_crease:

*************
Vertex Crease
*************

Adjusts the Crease value of the selected vertices, which determines the sharpness of their
corner when using the /modeling/modifiers/generate/subdivision_surface.

The Crease value can also be changed in the modeling-mesh-transform-panel
of the Sidebar.
