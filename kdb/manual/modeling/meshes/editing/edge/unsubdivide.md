************
Un-Subdivide
************

Removes edges in an attempt to undo a previous /modeling/meshes/editing/edge/subdivide operation.
If additional editing was done after the subdivide, unexpected results may occur.

Options
=======

Iterations
   How many subdivisions to remove.
