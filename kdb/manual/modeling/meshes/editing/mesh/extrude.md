*******
Extrude
*******

The available operators in this menu depend on the current selection.
Many of them are also available in the Vertex </modeling/meshes/editing/vertex/index>,
Edge </modeling/meshes/editing/edge/index>, and Face </modeling/meshes/editing/face/index> menus.

Extrude Faces
=============

Available when at least one face is selected.

See /modeling/meshes/editing/face/extrude_faces.

Extrude Faces Along Normals
===========================

Available when at least one face is selected.

See /modeling/meshes/editing/face/extrude_faces_normal.

Extrude Individual Faces
========================

Available when at least one face is selected.

See /modeling/meshes/editing/face/extrude_individual_faces.

Extrude Manifold
================

Available when at least one face is selected.

See /modeling/meshes/tools/extrude_manifold.

Extrude Edges
=============

Available when at least one edge is selected.

See /modeling/meshes/editing/edge/extrude_edges.

Extrude Vertices
================

Available when at least one vertex is selected.

See /modeling/meshes/editing/vertex/extrude_vertices.

.. _bpy.ops.mesh.extrude_repeat:

Extrude Repeat
==============

Repeatedly extrudes the selection along the current viewing direction, away from the
current viewpoint. Works best when using a top or side view </editors/3dview/navigate/viewpoint>
or a custom viewing direction with orthographic projection </editors/3dview/navigate/projections>.

Steps
   Number of extrusions.
Offset X, Y, Z
   Distance between each extrusion.
Scale Offset
   Multiplication factor for the *Offset*.
