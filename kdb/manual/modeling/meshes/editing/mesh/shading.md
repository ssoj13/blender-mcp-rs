*******
Shading
*******

Smooth/Flat Faces
=================

Applies smooth <bpy.ops.mesh.faces_shade_smooth> or
flat <bpy.ops.mesh.faces_shade_flat> shading to the selected faces.

Smooth/Sharp Edges
==================

Clears or applies the Sharp <bpy.ops.mesh.mark_sharp> mark for
the selected edges.

Smooth/Sharp Vertices
=====================

Clears or applies the Sharp <bpy.ops.mesh.mark_sharp> mark for
all edges that have at least one selected vertex.
