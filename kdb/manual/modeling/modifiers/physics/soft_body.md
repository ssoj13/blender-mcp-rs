******************
Soft Body Modifier
******************

The Soft Body modifier is a container for a Soft Body Physics </physics/soft_body/index> simulation.

Options
=======

As the modifier is only a container its actual options are configured in the *Physics Properties* tab.
See the Soft Body Physics Properties </physics/soft_body/settings/index> for more information.
