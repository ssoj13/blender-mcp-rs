**********************
Dynamic Paint Modifier
**********************

The Dynamic Paint modifier is a container for a Dynamic Paint Physics </physics/dynamic_paint/index> simulation.

Options
=======

As the modifier is only a container its actual options are configured in the *Physics Properties* tab.
See the Dynamic Paint Physics Properties </physics/dynamic_paint/index> for more information.
