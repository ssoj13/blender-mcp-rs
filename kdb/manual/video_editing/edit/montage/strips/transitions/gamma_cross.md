*****************
Gamma Cross Strip
*****************

The Gamma Cross transition is similar to the /video_editing/edit/montage/strips/transitions/cross transition,
however, the Gamma Cross strip transition uses color correction while transitioning between the two strips,
resulting in a smoother transition that is easier on the eyes.

Options
=======

Default Fade
   Automatically calculates a linear fade over the length of the strip.

   Effect Fader
      Allows you to manually keyframe </animation/keyframes/index> a custom fade.
      This can be used with different easings
      to fine-tune the fade in/out.
