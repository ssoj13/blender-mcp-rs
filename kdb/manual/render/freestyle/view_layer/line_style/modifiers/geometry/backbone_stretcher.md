.. _bpy.types.LineStyleGeometryModifier_BackboneStretcher:

******************
Backbone Stretcher
******************

The *Backbone Stretcher* modifier stretches (adds some length to)
the beginning and end of the stroke.

Backbone Length
   Length to add to the strokes' ends.
