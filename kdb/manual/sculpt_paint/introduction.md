************
Introduction
************

Sculpting and painting offers a more freeform workflow of editing via brushes </sculpt_paint/brush/index>.
There are several modes </editors/3dview/modes> to do this, each with their own purpose.

- Sculpting </sculpt_paint/sculpting/index>:
  Change and transform the topology of your mesh.
- Vertex Paint </sculpt_paint/vertex_paint/introduction>:
  Change the color of vertices in the active Color Attribute.
- Weight Paint </sculpt_paint/weight_paint/introduction>:
  Change the weight of vertices in the active vertex group.
- Texture Paint </sculpt_paint/texture_paint/introduction>:
  Change the pixels of the active image texture.
