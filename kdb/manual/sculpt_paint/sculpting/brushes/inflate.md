*******
Inflate
*******

Similar to Draw </sculpt_paint/sculpting/brushes/clay>,
except that vertices are moved in the direction of their own normals.
Especially useful when sculpting meshes with a lot of curvature.

Also available as a Mesh Filter </sculpt_paint/sculpting/tools/mesh_filter>
to inflate all unmasked areas at once.

Brush Settings
==============

General
-------

Direction
   Either Inflate or Deflate sculpted areas.
   This is different from the typical Add & Subtract.

**Note:**

   More info at sculpt-tool-settings-brush-settings-general brush settings
   and on sculpt-tool-settings-brush-settings-advanced brush settings.
